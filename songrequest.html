Let Me Make You A Song

This is for DJs, or anyone who likes listening to music. I'm always looking for inspiration and purpose to create new tracks.



To request a track please send me an email stating the title of the track to be created. (I'm very invested in what songs are called). I would also recommend including additional information too, like tempo, genre, mood, or how you intend to use the track. Technically just the title is a valid request tho ;) .



Availability permitting, I'll do this free of charge. You'll have exclusive access to the track for at least a month, after which I may choose to release it.
